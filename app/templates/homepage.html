What is StuyTCG?

StuyTCG is web-based trading card game (TCG) themed around Stuyvesant. Users will be able to pull cards, show off their collections, and battle other users using teacher cards from different departments. Cards will have 4 values: HP, Attack (or Special), Defense, and Speed. Users can also tour the card encyclopedia of 24 cards, where users can choose their starting 8 cards (they can choose at most 2 of a card). Out of those 8 cards, users will keep 5 on hand, one of which will be the active card in battle, and 3 cards in the deck. If a card has 0 or less HP remaining, that card is “KO’d” and goes into the user’s discard pile. The user must then select a new card from their hand to become their active card, and pull a new card from their deck into their hand. One side wins when the other player no longer has any cards left to play.


Gameplay

How To Play:

  • Each player has a deck of 8 cards. (The other player is a bot we don't have multiplayer sorry)
  • 1 card is active, 4 are in your hand, and the other 3 are "reserves".
  • Each turn, you can choose to either use your active card's move, or swap in a card from your hand to your active slot.
  • Certain cards have special moves, which have an effect other than simply dealing damage. As they are usually stronger than normal attacks, they can be used only one every other turn.
  • Once both players make their action, they get played out in order of speed, with higher speed cards going first. This also applies for swaps, where the speed used is the speed of the card swapping in.
  • For any damaging attacks, the damage dealt follows the following formula:

(atk stat of attacker + buffs of attacker) - (def stat of defender + buffs of defender)

  • When your active card's HP reaches 0, a reserve card will swap in for it. If you have no reserves left, you will simply need to swap out your card for one in your hand before continuing.
  • The game ends when one side has no more cards that can fight.
